Artificial cervical disc systems

ILT Spine Artificial Cervical Disc

An articulating artificial cervical disc designed to restore motion and function following disc removal. The ILT Artificial Disc enables natural movement at the cervical spine level, supporting long-term patient outcomes.

Advanced Solutions

Why Choose ILT Spine Artificial Disc

Mimics natural physiological motion

Designed to restore and maintain natural spinal motion patterns at the treated level.

Prosthesis follows the facet

Optimized alignment with the natural anatomy of the cervical spine.

Semi-constrained, ball and socket design with UHMWPE and 2 titanium endplates

Advanced materials and biomechanical design for durability and performance.

10° flexion/extension and unlimited rotation

Provides comprehensive range of motion for natural neck movement.

Works in concert with the rest of the motion segment

Integrates seamlessly with surrounding spinal structures for optimal function.

Ergonomically developed instruments

Precision surgical tools designed for ease of use and optimal outcomes.

Device Motion

Natural Movement & Flexibility

The Artificial Cervical Disc is engineered to mimic the natural motion of a healthy disc, allowing for flexion, extension, and unlimited rotation while maintaining stability and supporting the body's biomechanics.

Flexion & Extension

10° of forward and backward bending motion

Rotation

Unlimited rotational movement

Lateral Bending

Side-to-side motion preserved
